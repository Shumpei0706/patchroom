Patchroom's venue and device pixel illustrations were created with OpenAI ImageGen. The patch data, dialogue, music synthesis, signal rendering and sound design were created for Patchroom. Meeting voices are synthesized with Piper using en_US-ljspeech-high by Bryce Beattie. Model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/high/MODEL_CARD . Training data: the public-domain LJ Speech Dataset, https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/. The app contains two rendered lines of dialogue; it does not include the voice model.

The project was developed with Codex. Claude Code assisted with the submission film and AGY reviewed submission copy. No external AI service receives the user's CSV or runs the routing checks.
